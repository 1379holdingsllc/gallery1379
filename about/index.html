About Gallery 1379

Gallery 1379 is a small art brand from 1379 Holdings LLC, operating online and serving customers across the US. We started with a simple idea: some of the greatest paintings ever made are in the public domain, and they deserve a place on everyday walls, not only in museums.

Our print collection comes from The Metropolitan Museum of Art's Open Access program, which releases images of public-domain works under CC0. We choose pieces we love, from Duccio to Cézanne, and have each one printed to order on quality paper or canvas.

We also work with independent artists across the US to sell original work on consignment, and help businesses anywhere in the US buy or rent art for their spaces.
