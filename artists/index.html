Artist FAQ

What does it cost me?

Nothing upfront. No listing or submission fees. We earn a commission only when a piece sells.

What is the commission?

30–40% of the sale price, agreed with you in writing for each piece before it is listed. You keep the rest.

Is it exclusive?

No. Keep selling your work anywhere else, including your own shows and social media.

Who sets the price?

You do. We can share what we see selling, but the final price is yours.

How and when do I get paid?

After the buyer pays and the sale is final, we pay your share. Timing and method are written into your consignment agreement.

What work are you looking for?

Original paintings and mixed media from artists anywhere in the US, ready to hang. All styles welcome.

How do I get my work back?

It's your work. Ask any time and we arrange its return.

Who are you?

Gallery 1379 is a new, small gallery and print brand run online by 1379 Holdings LLC. We're building our artist roster now.
