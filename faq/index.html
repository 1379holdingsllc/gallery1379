Shipping, returns & FAQ

How do I order?

Pick a print, choose a format and size, and send the order request. We reply with your total including shipping and a secure payment link. Nothing is printed until you pay.

How much is shipping?

Shipping depends on the format and your address. We quote it in your total before you pay. We currently ship within the US.

How long does it take?

Prints are made to order, usually produced in 2–7 business days, then shipped. We'll give you an estimate with your quote.

What paper and canvas do you use?

Posters are printed on heavyweight enhanced matte archival paper. Canvas prints are gallery-wrapped on wooden stretcher bars. Framed posters come in black, white or oak wood frames with acrylic glazing.

Can I return a print?

Because each print is made to order, we can't accept returns for change of mind. If your order arrives damaged, defective or wrong, email a photo to 1379holdingsllc@gmail.com within 30 days of delivery and we'll send a replacement.

Can I cancel?

Yes, at no cost any time before you pay. After payment, contact us quickly; once production starts we may not be able to cancel.

Are these the original paintings?

No. They are reproductions of public-domain paintings in The Met collection, using CC0 Open Access images. Gallery 1379 is not affiliated with The Met.

Do you offer other sizes?

Ask us. We can often do other sizes or formats and will quote honestly.
